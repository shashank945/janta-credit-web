What is JantaCredit?

India is home to over 1.4 billion people, yet a large portion of the population still lacks access to formal credit systems. While digital finance has advanced rapidly in urban areas, rural and semi-urban communities continue to struggle with access to safe, reliable, and fair lending options. Informal moneylenders often exploit borrowers with extremely high interest rates and no legal protections, creating a vicious cycle of debt and poverty. Traditional banks, on the other hand, are reluctant to offer loans to individuals without a formal credit history, stable income, or collateral.

JantaCredit was built to bridge this gap. Our mission is to create an inclusive, community-powered lending ecosystem that empowers individuals—especially those from underserved communities—to access credit safely and responsibly. By leveraging digital technologies, peer verification through guarantors, and gamified credit-building tools, JantaCredit ensures that financial support is available to those who need it most.

In India, over 190 million adults remain unbanked, and even more are unable to access loans from regulated institutions due to poor or non-existent credit scores. Migrant workers, gig economy laborers, small-scale farmers, and women-led households often face the brunt of this exclusion. Even those who manage to open a bank account are not automatically included in the formal credit ecosystem.

The current financial infrastructure heavily favors individuals with existing credit history and formal income proofs—requirements that inherently exclude a large segment of India’s informal workforce. This disconnect not only restricts economic opportunities for individuals but also hampers the country's broader goals of financial inclusion and equitable development.

JantaCredit offers a platform where users can easily apply for micro-loans without traditional credit scores or collateral. Instead, our system is based on a community-guarantee model—borrowers submit the details of three guarantors (friends, family, or trusted individuals) who vouch for their credibility. This restores the social trust model into the digital finance world.

We also feature an intuitive EMI calculator, multilingual support, and UPI-based repayment to ensure the platform is simple and accessible. A dynamic dashboard helps users track their credit journey, view repayment schedules, and receive financial literacy tips.

JantaCredit doesn’t just stop at access—we believe in building long-term financial health. Users are rewarded for on-time repayments with badges and points through our gamified credit-building system, encouraging responsible borrowing behavior.

Our platform was created with one goal in mind: to democratize credit in India. We believe every person—regardless of their social status, income level, or geographic location—deserves the opportunity to grow financially. JantaCredit is more than a loan platform; it’s a step toward financial dignity for all.

In the long run, we envision JantaCredit as a trusted partner in rural and semi-urban India's financial journeys, helping millions escape exploitative lending practices and move toward self-reliance and prosperity.
